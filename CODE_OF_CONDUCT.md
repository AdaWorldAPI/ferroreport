# Code of conduct

ferroreport is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of
experience. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else
in the project's name, is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new;
- criticise code and ideas, never people;
- assume good faith, and ask before concluding someone meant harm;
- keep discussions about the project.

Not accepted: harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks,
publishing others' private information, and sexual language or imagery.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who don't follow
this code. To report a problem privately, write to cencarnacioninf13@gmail.com; reports are
kept confidential.
